# Contribution rules

- Preserve the pin map in `design/pin-map.md` and the wiring guide together.
- Treat `arduino/DigitalVotingMachine/DigitalVotingMachine.ino` as the firmware source of truth.
- Do not claim a test passed unless its command was executed and evidence was retained.
- Do not create placeholder `.dsn` or `.pdsprj` files.
- Keep voter identity and candidate choice separate in persistent data and audit output.
- This is an academic prototype; avoid production-election claims.
